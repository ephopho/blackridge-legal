Blackridge Academy: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Blackridge Academy is a naval academy life game that runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

The game saves a small amount of information locally on your iPhone or iPad so it can remember your progress:

  • Your saved game: where you are in your years at the academy, your cadet's skills, the choices you have made, and where you were standing. A backup copy is kept so a save can never be lost halfway through writing.
  • Two display settings: the graphics quality the game picked for your device, and whether it limits the frame rate to save battery.
  • The latest copy of the game's settings file (see Network below).

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network

Blackridge Academy works fully offline. When a connection is available it downloads one small, public settings file (game balance values and an optional news line) from GitHub Pages (ephopho.github.io) when it starts, and at most every 30 minutes after that. The request sends no personal information, identifiers, or game data, and we receive nothing from it. As with any web request, GitHub, which hosts the file, sees your IP address and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game makes no other network requests, and the game engine's analytics, crash reporting, performance reporting, and device statistics are all turned off.

Purchases

Blackridge Academy has no in-app purchases and no advertising.

Children

Blackridge Academy does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
