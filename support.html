Controls

  • Move: the stick at the bottom left.
  • Look: drag anywhere else on the screen.
  • JUMP (bottom right) changes with what you are doing: it becomes FIRE on the range, BRAKE on the rappel rope, and HAND OVER (stop the drill) in the man overboard drill.
  • DOUBLE keeps you at the double (a run) until you stop. Before you pass off the square, cadets move at the double anyway.
  • INTERACT appears near a person or an activity. You can also tap the words at the bottom of the screen.
  • SALUTE appears near officers and cadets you know. Some of them return it.

Common questions

  • How does a week work? Each week you have 40 hours to spend on training, study and the activities around the campus. When you are done, go to your bunk in the cadet lines to end the week and see how it went.
  • How do I save? The game saves by itself after your choices, at the end of every week, and whenever you leave the app. Tap Continue on the title screen to pick up where you left off.
  • How do I start again? Tap New Game on the title screen. If you already have a saved game, it asks you to confirm before it is replaced.
  • The game feels slow on my device. It lowers its own graphics detail if it can't keep a smooth frame rate, and remembers that for next time.
